## Certification Requirements

### NRSWA (New Roads and Street Works Act 1991)
- **Unit 2**: Site Setting Out (required for all operatives working in or near carriageway)
- **Unit 3**: Signing, Lighting, Guarding
- **Unit 4-8**: Reinstatement (various materials)
- **Supervisor Units (1, 3, 10, 12)**: Required for supervisors
- Issued by: City & Guilds, NOCN, or equivalent approved body
- Renewal: every 5 years

### HDD / Directional Drilling
- **IADC (International Association of Drilling Contractors)**: Drill operator certification
- **Highway Authority approval**: Bore plan submission and approval before drilling
- **Network Rail Sentinel**: Required for works near railway infrastructure

### Fiber Optic
- **City & Guilds 3667**: Fiber Optic Communication Technology
  - Unit 201: Install and terminate structured cabling
  - Unit 207: Install and terminate fiber optic cabling
  - Unit 208: Test and fault-find fiber optic cabling
- **ECS/JIB Fiber Card**: Industry ID card for fiber professionals
- Operator must hold valid OTDR test equipment qualification

### Gas Proximity
- **Gas Proximity Working**: Required for all contractors working within 3m of gas main
- Issued by: National Grid Gas / Cadent authorized training providers
- Covers: Gas escape recognition, emergency procedures, safe dig requirements

### High Voltage Electricity
- **DNO Approved**: Distribution Network Operator (e.g., UK Power Networks) must approve all HV contractors
- **ECS Gold Card (HV Jointer)**: City & Guilds HV jointing qualification
- **NICEIC / ECA Registration**: Electrical contractor registration

### Confined Space
- **City & Guilds 6150**: Confined Space Safety
- Required for: chamber entry, manhole work, any space with restricted entry/exit
- Includes: entrant, standby person, and rescue team certification

### Asbestos Awareness
- **HSE Asbestos Awareness (Cat A)**: For all workers who may disturb asbestos
- Required in all demolition/excavation work on pre-2000 buildings/infrastructure
